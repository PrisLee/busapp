    What this does

    Every bus app shows you a countdown. None of them tell you whether that countdown can be trusted. This one keeps a record of when buses actually arrive at the stops outside CT Hub 2, then uses it to work out how much slack to leave when you walk out of the building.

    1. Pick where you’re going.
    2. Get a leave-by time — the lift out of the building, your walk, and a margin for how early this bus tends to turn up.
    3. See what it costs to miss it, based on the gaps we’ve actually recorded.

    Maps and full route browsing: BusRouter SG. Live arrivals from LTA DataMall.
